About us

People are the heart of every good match.

Margaret Gituto Recruitment brings employers and professionals together with practical, personal support at every stage of the journey.

Our approach

Recruitment that looks beyond the résumé.

Finding the right person takes more than matching a title to a CV. We take time to understand what employers need and help professionals communicate the value they bring.

From sourcing and shortlisting through interviews, onboarding and post-placement support, we stay close to the process. For job seekers, we offer practical tools—from CVs and cover letters to LinkedIn profiles—that help make their next step clearer.
